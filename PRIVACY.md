# Sahaay - Privacy Policy (prototype)

Sahaay is a student hackathon project (PromptWars x Catalyst, Hack2Skill). This policy covers the prototype at the repository's deployed demo URL.

## What we store
- Care profile data you enter: the elderly person's first name, family member names and roles, email addresses for reminders, medication schedules (names, dosages, times), appointments, and dose history.
- A 4-8 digit family passcode per profile, stored as a one-way hash.

## What we do with it
- Send transactional email reminders (upcoming/missed doses, appointments) to the email addresses that profile members entered themselves.
- Show the shared schedule and history to anyone who joins the profile with its passcode.

## What we never do
- No marketing email, no sale or sharing of data with third parties, no advertising, no tracking cookies.
- No medical advice. Schedules are entered by the family; Sahaay never infers doses or treatments.

## Recipients
Emails go only to addresses that members typed into the app when creating or joining a profile. There is no purchased, scraped, or imported contact list.

## Deletion
This is a demo prototype. To remove a profile and its data, archive the schedules and contact the maintainer via the GitHub repository issues page.
